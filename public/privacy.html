Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gainline ("Gainline," "we," "us") collects through the Gainline website and app (the "Service"), how it's used, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: name, username, email address, and password (stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash — we never store or can see your actual password).

Profile and coaching content: bio text, workout and diet sheets, check-in submissions (including any photos or videos you choose to upload), messages sent to your coach or clients, notes a coach keeps on a client (visible only to that coach), calendar events, and reviews.

Payment information: Gainline never sees or stores your card number or other payment details — those are entered directly with Stripe, our payment processor. We do store records of transactions (amounts, dates, which coach/client a payment relates to) and Stripe-issued identifiers needed to process payments and payouts.

Usage information: basic technical data collected automatically, like IP address and timestamps, used for security (e.g. rate-limiting abuse) and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to: operate and provide the Service (connecting coaches and clients, delivering sheets/check-ins/messages); process payments and payouts through Stripe; send account-related emails (email verification, password reset); respond to support requests you submit; maintain security and prevent abuse (e.g. bot signups); and improve the Service.

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Service:

The other party in a coaching relationship: a coach and their connected client(s) can see the sheets, check-ins, messages, and calendar events relevant to that relationship. Private notes a coach writes about a client are visible only to that coach, never to the client, and never carry over to a different coach if the client switches.

Service providers we use to run Gainline: Stripe (payment processing and payouts), Resend (transactional email delivery), and our hosting/infrastructure providers (currently Render and Cloudflare). Each only receives the information it needs to perform its function, and each has its own privacy practices.

Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Gainline or our users.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, and afterward only as needed for legitimate business or legal purposes (e.g. financial records). You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it — sheets, check-ins, messages, notes, payment plans, calendar data — at any time from your dashboard. This is immediate and cannot be undone. If you have any other request about your data, use the "Contact support" link on the site.

5. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored or logged in plain text. All traffic to the Service is encrypted (HTTPS). Check-in videos and photos are stored privately and only accessible to the client who submitted them and their coach — never served as public files. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your information.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users 18 years of age or older, consistent with our Terms of Use. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Your Choices

You can review and update your profile information from your dashboard at any time, unsubscribe from any non-essential email, and delete your account (and all associated data) whenever you choose, as described in Section 4.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through the "Contact support" link available throughout the site.
